# Vancouver Vice gap matrix

What players expect from the genre, ranked by what they touch in the first ten minutes, then by depth systems.

| Rank | System | What players expect | Which games do it well | Size | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Walking, running and jumping feel | Movement should feel quick and weighted, with clean turns, sharp stops and easy steps over low things. | GTA V, Sleeping Dogs | S | have |
| 2 | Driving feel | Cars should steer with weight, brake hard and slide a little without feeling like toys or bricks. | Sleeping Dogs, GTA V | M | have |
| 3 | Camera | The camera should sit behind the player on foot and in cars, swing on its own and never snap or fight the player. | GTA V, Watch Dogs | S | have |
| 4 | Tutorial and control prompts | A short opening teaches moves, driving and the first job without a wall of text. | GTA V, Sleeping Dogs | S | missing |
| 5 | HUD | Health, cash, wanted stars and the current job sit on screen, small and easy to read. | GTA V, Sleeping Dogs | S | missing |
| 6 | Minimap and waypoints | A minimap shows roads, nearby blips and a route to the next job. | Sleeping Dogs, GTA V | M | missing |
| 7 | Wanted level and police response | Crimes seen by people raise heat, cops respond, and breaking line of sight and hiding cools it down. | GTA V, Red Dead Redemption 2 | L | web only |
| 8 | Gunplay with aim, ammo and reloads | Guns should feel punchy, aim should be clear, magazines run out and reloading takes a real beat. | GTA V, Watch Dogs | L | web only |
| 9 | Melee punch and brawls | A punch should land with feedback, knock people down and work as a no-gun option. | Sleeping Dogs, Saints Row | S | web only |
| 10 | Death screen and respawn | Dying shows a clear screen, costs a little money or time, and drops the player at a hospital or safe spot. | GTA V, Sleeping Dogs | S | web only |
| 11 | Pedestrians reacting | People should run, scream, film or dodge when shot at or hit, and not just walk fixed paths. | GTA IV, GTA V | M | missing |
| 12 | Traffic that reacts to the player | Cars should brake, swerve, honk or pile up when the player crashes into them, and stop at lights. | GTA V, Watch Dogs | M | missing |
| 13 | Radio stations | Driving should bring a radio with several stations, songs and DJ or talk breaks. | GTA V, the first GTA (1997) | M | missing |
| 14 | Phone | A phone gives texts, contacts, job alerts and a way to call a car or a friend. | GTA V, Watch Dogs | M | missing |
| 15 | Full map and fast travel | A full map shows the whole region, lets players set waypoints and jump between places they have visited. | GTA V, Sleeping Dogs | M | missing |
| 16 | Saving | Players can save at a bed, safehouse or phone and come back to the same place after quitting. | GTA V, Cyberpunk 2077 | S | missing |
| 17 | Money, shops and walk-in buildings | Cash should come in and go out, with shops, a garage and buildings the player can walk into and buy from. | GTA V, Sleeping Dogs | L | web only |
| 18 | Story missions and variety | Missions should change shape, with chases, stealth and boss fights, and the build has only two so far. | Mafia, GTA V | M | have |
| 19 | Side activities and free-roam jobs | Small jobs and side tasks should show as markers on the map and pay out, tied into the wider world. | Sleeping Dogs, GTA V | M | web only |
| 20 | Heists and multi-stage jobs | Planned jobs should have a setup, a sequence of steps, a getaway and a clear fail state. | GTA V | L | web only |
| 21 | Day and night cycle | Time should pass, lights should come on at night, and traffic and people should change with the hour. | GTA V, Sleeping Dogs | M | missing |
| 22 | Weather | Rain should wet the roads, change driving and change how the city looks. | GTA IV, GTA V | M | missing |
| 23 | Police AI tiers | Heat should escalate from patrol cars to roadblocks, air units and SWAT, with each tier feeling harder. | GTA V, Watch Dogs | L | web only |
| 24 | Vehicle damage | Crashes should dent, smoke, break glass and stop engines, so a wreck looks and drives like one. | GTA IV, GTA V | M | missing |
| 25 | Vehicle roster and garages | A mix of cars, bikes and trucks, with a garage to keep or swap them, should make each drive feel different. | GTA V, Sleeping Dogs | M | missing |
| 26 | Character customization | Clothes, haircuts and tattoos should matter and be bought at shops. | GTA V, Saints Row | M | missing |
| 27 | Ambient street crime | Crimes, crashes and fights should happen without a job, and the player can stop them or join in. | GTA V | M | missing |
| 28 | Safehouses and property | A home base should let players save, stash items and change clothes. | GTA V | M | missing |
| 29 | Sea, boats and water | Water should have boats, swimming and a feel that matches the land. | GTA V | M | web only |
| 30 | Online play | Friends should be able to join, race and cause trouble together. | GTA Online | L | web only |
