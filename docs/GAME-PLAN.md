# Game plan

The whole game, from today to a release friends play for real. Each milestone ships something you can play and show. Story beats come from STORY.md; systems from the roadmap.

## 1. You, in Vancouver (next session)
Joshua looks like Joshua: male build, fair skin on the arms, a proper polo collar, slim jeans, short strawberry-blond curls. He stands on a real sidewalk at the Pacific Centre Apple Store, because the Granville and Georgia detail island is imported over the Google tiles. A splash screen holds until the tiles around him are loaded. Done when a full-body photo goes to your brother without a joke.

## 2. Mission one plays (the first five minutes)
Run out of the Apple Store with the Mac minis. Two employees in blue shirts chase you; the game teaches aim and fire. Jack the car at the curb. One star, a siren. Lose the cops past the Art Gallery. Drive to Granville and pawn the minis. Walk cycle, car driving, cops, stars, minimap, the cash counter. Done when autoplay runs it start to finish and records a video.

## 3. The game around it
Title screen with New Game, Continue, Settings, Quit. Save and load (position, mission, cash, stars, autosave on mission complete). Settings for tile detail, audio, controls. Live Vancouver weather from Open-Meteo. Radio in the car.

## 4. Act one finishes (missions 2 to 5)
The debt: Dmitri, the crypto guy in a Patagonia vest in a fake Gastown co-working space, wants forty grand by Friday. Gastown, Robson at Burrard and Waterfront get detail islands. Drive-by shooting from the car. Pursuit cars, roadblocks at three stars.

## 5. Act two: Ben and Alexandre
Switch heroes. Ben in Kits: Dad's boat, the Lululemon sale stampede, the delivery van. Alexandre in Victoria: the Swartz Bay ferry, Government Street, the presale money trail. Ben and Alexandre as MetaHumans from face scans; Brian, Christine and Sarah too.

## 6. Act three: the heist
Dad's garage, Mom's sandwiches. Pick loud (armoured truck on the Lions Gate) or quiet (presale party as realtors). Sarah joins after you drive her to a job interview in Burnaby on time. The score, the getaway under the bridge to North Van, two endings, family dinner, the hard drive returned.

## 7. Open world and release
Side jobs: bike lane rage, seagulls stealing your hot dog at English Bay, SkyTrain surfing, the film crew prop cop car, Tesla drag races, a hockey riot. Fast travel to Victoria, Seattle, Toronto, New York. Package the Mac .app (unreal/package.sh), autoplay QA on the packaged build, trademark filing, Steam page, a Windows build on a rented PC.

## What makes it go faster
An external NVMe SSD for the engine and project (boots from 15 minutes to about 2). The live MetaHuman preview before any build. One milestone per session, ending with a photo or a video.
