# Mission One: Out the Door

You start at the Pacific Centre Apple Store on Georgia with a bag of Mac minis. Two employees in blue shirts charge you across the sidewalk. One's calling the cops.

Your gun is loaded. Mouse aims, click fires. Keep them off the bag. They grab it, you lose.

A sports car idles at the curb fifty meters south. Run for it. E gets you in.

The moment you're driving, a siren hits. One star. The cops are coming. Step on it, break their line of sight, lose the star.

Next up: drive-by shooting from the car while escaping the cops. That comes later once the heat system is live.
